Laundry Care Information

Care starts with clear instructions.

How to prepare your laundry, and how we care for it.

Before pickup

  • Empty all pockets.
  • Bag your laundry securely.
  • Flag any items with special care labels.
  • Tell us about special items before pickup.

Preferences

Tell us your detergent preference (such as regular or fragrance-free), drying preference (regular or low heat) and any special garment instructions when you book. We confirm which requests we can accommodate before washing.

Special items

Start with everyday machine-washable clothing. Machine-washable bedding and regular towel pickups are quote requests, subject to acceptance. Ask for a quote.

Stains & results

We take care with every load, but we can’t promise complete stain removal, sterilization or allergy-safe results.

Policies to come

Owner to supply The owner will confirm these before launch: whether each order is washed separately, items we can’t accept, how stains are handled, and how to report a lost or damaged item.
