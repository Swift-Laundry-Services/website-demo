How Swift works

We pick up your laundry, personally wash it at local laundromats, then fold, package and return it to your door.

  • Convenient pickups

    We pick up your laundry at your door, on a schedule that works for you.

  • Care preferences welcome

    Tell us how you like your laundry, and we’ll do our best to follow your requests.

  • Clear communication

    We’ll keep you updated about your pickup, washing and delivery.
